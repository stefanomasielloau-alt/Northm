# North video plan and scripts (2026-10-10)

**Brief (yours):** simple, not an animated explainer. "People" using it, collaborating, seeking results. A 2-minute overview, then a few 1–2 minute feature dives. Low cost.

## 1. Can I produce it? Straight answer
- **I can fully prepare it:** script, shot list, per-clip prompts for AI video tools, caption text, on-screen text, a recording checklist, and the edit plan.
- **I cannot generate or film people myself.** I have no video-generation tool here, and I won't fake the product: screens in the video must be real captures of North (never AI-drawn interfaces), because showing a feature that doesn't exist would undermine the whole "credibility" message.
- **What I could assemble with your go-ahead:** a rough cut from real North screenshots (taken in your signed-in browser pane), with captions, Ken-Burns motion and a voiceover track, using ffmpeg here. It would be silent-film-quality product footage, not a finished promo, but it gives a real baseline to drop people footage into. (The tools to do this are installed here; I have not built it.)
- **Your decision on people footage:** pick one of the options in section 2.

Keep the voiceover human if you can (your own voice, recorded on a phone, is more credible than synthetic); a clearly-disclosed AI voice is acceptable for the feature dives.

**Checklist before publishing (6 items):** (1) written releases for anyone on camera; (2) commercial-use terms of every tool and footage source saved to a file; (3) music from a licensed library only; (4) AI-generated footage disclosed in the description; (5) no client logos or real customer data on screen without permission — use a demo organisation; (6) captions on every video (accessibility, and most people watch muted).

### AI-video prompts for the P scenes (if you choose Option A)
Keep every prompt generic — no brand, logo, real person or recognisable character; ask for 8-second shots, neutral clothing, no legible text on screens (so nothing needs to match your real UI). Examples:
1. "Medium shot, modern meeting room, four professionals around a table, one holds a printed report and looks unconvinced, another gestures while explaining. Natural light, handheld, realistic, no text visible."
2. "Over-the-shoulder shot of a marketing manager at a laptop with several overlapping windows, rubbing their eyes, late afternoon office light, realistic, no readable text."
3. "Two colleagues review a budget together at a desk, one points at the laptop screen, both nod, warm light, realistic, screen content out of focus."
4. "Three colleagues from different teams lean toward one monitor, one smiles, shared relief, bright modern office, realistic, screen not readable."
Generate 3–4 takes per scene; pick the one with the fewest visual glitches; add a small "AI-generated footage" credit.

## 4. Feature dives (each 90 seconds, ~210 words, same visual key)

**Dive 1 — Plan to pipeline (≈90 s)**
1. S Strategy plan and targets. "Start with the plan: where the revenue has to come from, region by region."
2. S Create a campaign from the plan; add activities and budget lines. "Build campaigns that serve that plan. Add activities and cost lines, and the budget adds up automatically against your envelope."
3. S Link a deal to a campaign in Scoring. "When a deal arrives, link it to the campaign that sourced it."
4. S Effectiveness: cost per outcome, click through to the campaign. "Effectiveness shows what each campaign cost and what it delivered."
5. T "From plan to closed revenue." 
**Dive 2 — Connect your systems (≈90 s)** `[VERIFY: depends on a connector being tested live; until then show the connector cards and a Process Maps flow only]`

3. S Send test to me. "Send a test to yourself and check it before real recipients see it." `[only once the email provider is set up]`
4. T "Fewer copy-paste jobs. One source of truth."
**Dive 3 — Bring your own AI (≈90 s)**
1. S Configuration → AI providers. "Choose which AI providers your organisation approves, with your own keys."
2. S Fill from a brief. "Drop in a brief. North drafts the plan fields for you."

4. T "AI drafts. People decide."
**Dive 4 — The board report (≈90 s)**
1. S Board report page. "Everything the board asks for on one page."
2. S Charts: plan vs actual, pipeline by stage, gap to target. "Plan vs actual, pipeline by stage, and the gap to target."
3. S Arrange layout; PowerPoint export; access control. "Arrange it your way, export to PowerPoint, and control who can see it."
4. T "Take it to the board."
